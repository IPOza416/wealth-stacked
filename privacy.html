Privacy Policy

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Settings such as your theme, currency symbol, favorites and last-used inputs are stored in your browser’s local storage and never leave your device.

Market news

The Market News page loads public RSS headlines through a news proxy run by this site (a Cloudflare Worker), which receives your IP address and request metadata like any web server. If the proxy is not configured or is unavailable, the page falls back to the rss2json.com service, which then receives your IP address and the feed addresses requested. Opening a headline takes you to the publisher’s site, which has its own policy.

Hosting and analytics

Your host may keep standard server logs. If you add analytics or advertising, update this policy and obtain consent where required.
