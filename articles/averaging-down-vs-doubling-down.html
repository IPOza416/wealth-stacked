Averaging Down: When It Helps and When It Hurts

A lower average cost is not the same as a better investment.

Averaging down means buying more as the price falls, which lowers your average cost per share. It can make sense when your original thesis is intact and the position is still within your size limits.

It hurts when you add to a broken thesis, because you increase exposure to the same mistake.

Before you add, decide the maximum total position size, and ask whether you would buy the stock fresh today at this price.
