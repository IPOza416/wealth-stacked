Money & Stock Glossary

APR
Annual percentage rate: the yearly cost of borrowing including fees.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly rate that links a start and end value.
Dividend yield
Annual dividend divided by share price.
DRIP
Dividend reinvestment plan: dividends automatically buy more shares.
DTI
Debt-to-income: monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income.
EPS
Earnings per share: net profit divided by shares outstanding.
FIRE
Financial independence, retire early.
Margin of safety
The discount between a stock price and your estimate of its fair value.
P/E ratio
Price divided by earnings per share.
PEG ratio
P/E divided by expected earnings growth rate.
Position size
How many shares or how much money you put into one trade.
Rebalancing
Buying and selling to return a portfolio to target percentages.
Stop-loss
A preset price at which you exit a trade to limit the loss.
Withdrawal rate
The share of a portfolio you spend each year in retirement.
Yield on cost
Current dividend divided by your original purchase price.
